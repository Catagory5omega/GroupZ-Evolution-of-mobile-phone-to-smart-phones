6. Conclusion - (Synthesis and broader implications)

   example conclusion from tutorial:
   Conclusion
The transformation from Microsoft Office to Microsoft 365 represents a
microcosm of enterprise computing's evolution. Through the Business Viewpoint,
we see a masterful strategic pivot that created sustainable competitive
advantage and shareholder value. Through the Societal Viewpoint, we observe
both democratisation of powerful tools and concerning dependencies that affect
education, work, and equity.
This case demonstrates three critical lessons for enterprise computing:
1. Business success and social benefit don't automatically align, conscious
effort is required
2. Cloud transformation changes not just technology but fundamental social
structures
3. Ethical considerations must be embedded from the start, not retrofitted
As future computing professionals, we must learn from this evolution to create
solutions that are not only profitable and efficient but also equitable and
sustainable. The Microsoft 365 story reminds us that with great computational
power comes great social responsibility.
The significance extends beyond one company's product strategy, it exemplifies
how enterprise computing shapes modern life, making it essential to consider
multiple viewpoints and ethical implications in every development decision.
