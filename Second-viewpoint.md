The evolution of mobile phones to smartphones made an outstanding change to our society. For a device as light as a roll of quarters, more than 60% of the population owns a smartphone. ### Growth in Society:
An average smartphone owner as of 2026, touches their phone around 2,600 times a day, and from 2012 to 2026 currently, 48% of the average time spent on a smartphone jumped to 5-6 hours daily from the average smartphone user. This statistic shows an incredible growth in the social impact a smartphone has grown into society.

There are currently more than 7.58 billion smartphones in use today and over 97% of the users are between the ages of 18 and 49 years of age. This Proves that smartphones are the most used device currently.

### Impact on social skills:
The impact of the development of smartphones has allowed a drastic change on society's soacial skills. with both positives and negatives, the impact is great. The development of smartphones has lead to many people choosing to send a text to instigate conversation with others rather than meeting in person and doing so. Although this can be very helpful in mkost situations, it has led to a drastic decrease in social skills of most people because they would much prefer sending a text, rather than speaking in person.

 As of 2017 until now, study shows that smartphones have negatively affected social skills of many people. When people would have a conversation in person when they mostly engage in conversation online, they are then left vulnerable to social cues like body language, visual cues, and of course, facial expressions. Another negative can lead to the lack of patience your average person can have. The expectation of recieving a response immediately once texting, can leave others to expect that response time in other areas of life.

 There are also many positives involving social skills. You can increase your social skills using smartphones by calling rather than textoing when possible, which can increase social skills in other areas.
