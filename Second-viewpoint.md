# Second Viewpoint:

The evolution of mobile phones to smartphones made an outstanding change to our society. For a device as light as a roll of quarters, more than 60% of the population owns a smartphone. Ever since 2012, society started relying on smartphones as much as they would rely on any other every-day object, like a toothbrush, a fork, or a pen/pencil. the average amount opf hours on a mobile phone was 1 hour and 38 minutes, while when the smartphone was created, the average time spent on a smartphone almost doubled and jumped to 3 hours and 15 minutes. 

An average smartphone owner as of 2026, touches their phone around 2,600 times a day, and from 2012 to 2026 currently, 48% of the average time spent on a smartphone jumped to 5-6 hours daily from the average smartphone user. This statistic shows an incredible growth in the social impact a smartphone has grown into society.
