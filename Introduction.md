## Introduction
### Case study: Evolution of mobile phone to smart phone

This case study will examine the development of mobile phone to smartphones, providing an indept analysis of its historical developments and the key figures, major milestones that have contributed to the evolution of the modern mobile phones. This development will be analyse through two viewpoints, the viewpoint of business and societal, these viewpoints will explore how the evolution of mobile technologies have influence businesses and people's daily lives, including their contrasting perspectives on these development.

Before phones became publicly availible,radio communication was mainly used by the military and police, despite its bulkiness and how its not accessible to ordinary people, this system allows people to communicate over long distance, as demands increased manufacturers began to produce smaller, cheaper and more accessible devices.

Despite these advancements, the production of mobile phone have raised many important ethical and environmental concerns. Emphasizing the importance of understanding the wider impact of technological developments and the ethical responsibilities involed in manufacturing mobile phones


##### Historical development
The development of mobile phones have been shaped by a range of significant technological milestones, beginning with early mobile telephone systems in the 1940s, During its early stages, mobile communication devices was limited by the size and cost, making them accessible to only a small number of people.However as mobile technology continues to develop, improvements in cellular networks and digital communication gradually transfromed the way people used mobile phones. the introduction of IBM(International Business Machine) Simon Personal Communicator in 1994 further demonstrated the potential of combining traditional mobile phones features with computing capabilities, This section examine these developments and the key milestones that have contributed to the evolution of mobile phones into smartphones

##### First viewpoint: businesses
From a buissness perspective, the evolution of mobile phone can be analysed through Factors such as market trends, growing competitons , the rate of social economic growth within a country, supply and demand as well as the rapid growth of technology dependence in the business world, these factors influence how companies compete, develop new products and respond to changes in consumer demand 

##### Second viewpoint: societal
With the evolution of mobile phone to smart phones, it have become apparent that smartphones have become an essential part of our everyday life, this technology have changed the way people live, how we communicate and interact with the world, which have increased societies dependances on smartphones. it has also made accessing information, communication and education more convenient and accessible to anyone. Ultimately, despite these benefits, the growing relience on smartphones have raised concerns about their influence on traditional societal interations(ABERDEEN RESEARCH, N.D).

##### Ethical refelection
The ethical and envirnmental concerns that should be highlighted includes: the use of conflict minerals, poor labour conditions during production and the growing problem of electronic waste(The Good SHopping Guide,N.D). while the development of smartphones leads to improvements in communications and technology, the prodcution of smartphones requires raw materials, some of which are sourced from wartorn areas, the trade of these materials have contributed to violence and fund armed groups. Additionally workers involved in the production process can experience exploition, unsafe working conditions and low pay.the rapid replacement and disposal of smartphones have also contributed to the growing problem of electronic waste, which have a negative affect on human health and the environment.
